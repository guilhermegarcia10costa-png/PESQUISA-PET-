Shipping & Delivery

We ship worldwide, tracked. Standard delivery 6 to 10 working days to most countries (exact window shown by country at checkout). Personalised items add 2 to 4 working days for finishing. Free delivery over $40, otherwise $4.95. Tracking emailed on dispatch.

Returns & Exchanges

30-day fit guarantee: if the size is wrong, we swap it free, keep the first one on until the new one arrives. Unused non-personalised items refundable within 30 days. Personalised items only replaced if faulty.

About Dear Bron

Dear Bron is named after Bron, one of our two dogs. Every harness we tried on him rubbed, let him slip out, or frayed within a season, so we made one kit, sized properly, with their name on it.
